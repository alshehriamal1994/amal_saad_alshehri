Latest publication

A paper worth highlighting properly

2026 Artificial Intelligence and Law Open access

Neural reranking for UK statutory retrieval: Provision-level evaluation and an open distilled model

This work introduces UK-STATUTECORPUS and Distilled-Voyage-ModernBERT for provision-level statutory retrieval. It shows how proprietary legal rerankers can be distilled into open models that retain strong retrieval performance without depending on closed APIs.
