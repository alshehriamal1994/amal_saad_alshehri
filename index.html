Neural reranking for UK statutory retrieval: Provision-level evaluation and an open distilled model

Published in Artificial Intelligence and Law (Springer)

We introduce UK-STATUTECORPUS and Distilled-Voyage-ModernBERT to improve statutory retrieval. By distilling proprietary legal rerankers into open models, we achieve high-performance provision-level retrieval without relying on closed APIs.
